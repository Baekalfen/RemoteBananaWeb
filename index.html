Your Mac's hidden high-performance mode, on the iPad

macOS ships a High Performance Screen Sharing mode: hardware-accelerated, low-latency remote desktop with audio. Remote Banana speaks that protocol natively — video and audio stream as hardware-encoded media over encrypted UDP, not as a slow framebuffer redraw like VNC. Motion is smooth, audio comes along, and it stays sharp at your iPad's full Retina resolution. One-time purchase, no account, no cloud — a direct, encrypted connection on your own network.

What you get

01 Native sharpness

Your Mac renders a virtual display matching the iPad — pixel for pixel, even when you rotate.

02 Audio, too

Mac audio streams alongside the video, in sync, over the same encrypted connection.

03 One-tap connect

Configurations are auto-discovered on your network and unlock with Face ID. Tap, and you're on the desktop.

04 Built for touch

Three modes: direct drag, direct scroll, and a full touchpad with fling. Corner menu for everything else.

05 Apple Pencil

The Pencil works as a precise stylus on the remote Mac — with pressure-aware taps.

06 A real keyboard

On-screen or Bluetooth — modifiers, function keys, arrows, Esc and Tab all behave like on a Mac.

07 Picture-in-Picture

Minimize Remote Banana and the session keeps running in a PiP window while you use other apps.

08 Live Activity

Your connection lives in the Dynamic Island and Notification Center with status and duration at a glance.

Requirements

A Mac with Apple Silicon running macOS 14 or later, with Screen Sharing enabled in System Settings → General → Sharing. Both devices on the same network (TCP 5900 and UDP 5900–5902 reachable).

Away from home? Run Tailscale (or any VPN) on both devices and enter the Mac's Tailscale address (100.x.y.z or *.ts.net) as the host — no extra setup needed in the app.
